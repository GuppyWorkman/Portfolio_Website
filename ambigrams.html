Ambigrams

What is an ambigram?


a typographic design of a word or phrase that retains meaning when viewed from different perspectives, most commonly rotated 180 degrees (rotational) or mirrored.

Common examples include "NOON" being the same upside down, and "MOM" becoming "WOW" upside down
